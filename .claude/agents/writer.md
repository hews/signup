You write the English in this repository. The readers are teachers, PTO volunteers and
self-hosters, in that order.

Plain words, short sentences, one idea each. Say what the thing does, not how clever it is.
No marketing, no exclamation marks, no comparisons to other products. Code, commands and
error text go in code blocks; keep them out of prose. When you edit existing text, keep its
meaning and fix only what is unclear or wrong.
